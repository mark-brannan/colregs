# Timeline

When COLREG 1972 changed, and how long each jurisdiction took to catch up.
Public record, every row cited. Relevant to this package because
`jurisdiction` (`intl`, `us/inland`, …) is a first-class dimension here, not
an afterthought — this is the evidence that national texts genuinely lag
the IMO instrument, by years, and by different amounts per country.

| 2007-11-29 → 2009-12-01 | 2007 amdts, Res. A.1004(25) | Annex IV (distress signals) replaced wholesale: added DSC, Inmarsat, EPIRB signals | [imo.org](https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/safety/pages/preventing-collisions.aspx) | verified |
| 2013-12-04 → 2016-01-01 | 2013 amdts, Res. A.1085(28) | New Part F (Rules 39–41) appended: compulsory Member State Audit Scheme verification | [imo.org](https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/safety/pages/preventing-collisions.aspx) | verified |

No amendment adopted since 2013; autonomous-ship ("MASS") work is ongoing but has not produced one — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convention_on_the_International_Regulations_for_Preventing_Collisions_at_Sea).

**Renumbering:** none of the seven amendments renumbers or deletes an existing rule; each adds a paragraph, a definition, or (2013) an appended Part. Annex IV's 2007 replacement kept its number and position.

**Languages:** Article IX makes English and French "equally authentic"; Russian and Spanish are official translations, not authentic text — [Convention text, CIL NUS](https://cil.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/1972-Convention-on-Regulations-for-Preventing-Collisions-at-Sea.pdf), verified. IMO's own official languages (for documents generally, not this treaty) are Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian, Spanish — [imo.org](https://www.imo.org/en/About/Conventions/Pages/COLREG.aspx), verified.

## 3. Cadence

- **IMO gap between amendments:** seven amendments, 1981–2013 — 6y, 2y, 4y, 8y (max, 1993→2001), 6y, 6y. Mean ≈5.3y. None since 2013.
- **National lag varies by country and by amendment, not on a fixed schedule.** Finland and Australia both brought the 2007 amendments into force on 1 Dec 2009 — the same day as the global date. The U.S. did not incorporate the 2001 and 2007 amendments until an August 2014 rule: 13- and 7-year lags on the same two rounds.
- **Gazette date can trail legal effect.** Spain's 2007-amendment text was published in the BOE on 20 Mar 2010, ~3.5 months after it was already binding (1 Dec 2009) — a publishing lag, not two rule-numbering schemes concurrently in force. No case of the latter was found (absence not proven).
- **Citation style differs by country:** Spain and Finland cite the IMO resolution number and dates directly; the U.S. and Canada cite Federal Register/SOR numbers plus a "current to" date; the UK cites an SI number plus an MSN amendment; CEVNI cites a revision plus amendment number.
